# "You'll hear this" pack: batch 3 draft (30 cards)

Status: **fact-checked draft, waiting for the owner's review.** After your marks it becomes
`src/data/seedPack3.ts`, shipped with a new database version, marked as editor-written like the demo
cards.

How to review: for each card, check that it's right, that the top three lines (Means, When people
say it, Say back) give nothing away, and that it's a line your circle actually says. Mark a card
**cut** or **change** and say how; everything else ships as written.

Each card has:

- **Means**: what it means when someone says it.
- **When people say it**: what the speaker is doing with it.
- **Say back**: a short line you can reply with.
- **Story**: where it comes from.
- **Context**: how people use it now.
- **Example**: a real-life moment.

Spoiler-heavy cards keep the safe meaning first. The spoiler meaning is hidden until you choose to
see it, and its "When people say it" line ends with "save the scene until you've seen it".

Mix: Disney and Pixar 4 · Marvel 3 · Harry Potter 3 · Friends, The Office and other sitcoms 5 ·
memes 6 · Indian films, shows and memes 9. None repeat the 60 cards already in the app, and none
reuse their search words.

## Notes for the reviewer

- **Fact-checked once** by a separate checker with web lookups, and fixed:
  - card 5 ("I understood that reference") is from The Avengers (2012), not Age of Ultron;
  - the first draft's "Slimy yet satisfying" was swapped out: it is Simba's line, not Pumbaa's, and
    people rarely quote it, so card 30 (Uri) took its place;
  - card 13 (Kevin's few words) is from season 8 (2011), so its era is 2010s & later, and it is a
    one-episode idea, not how Kevin always talks;
  - card 7: in Ragnarok the Hulk is Thor's arena opponent, not an arriving teammate;
  - card 9 ("Constant vigilance!") is Moody's catchphrase in the Goblet of Fire book; a film blog says
    he never says it in the 2005 film, so the card is labelled as the books, not the film;
  - card 4: Nick greets Flash with the line, then tells a riddle (not "sets up a joke");
  - card 15: three knocks then "Penny", repeated three times;
  - card 17: the aliases "gru's plan" and "grus plan" were the same once the apostrophe is removed;
    the example now follows the real template (last panel repeats the third);
  - card 18: the figure is Meme Man, described without that name (it is already a search word on the
    Stonks card);
  - card 28 (Pawri): "cute accent" could read as mocking, so it now says "copying the viral clip",
    and the story names the creator and the remix;
  - cards 25 and 26: the story wording was tightened (the speech is about making seventy minutes
    count; "pyaar dosti hai" is said in a classroom scene).
  Confirmed: Mine (Nemo, 2003), Dug, Buzz's "sad, strange little man", "We have a Hulk", the Ron
  butterflies line (film only), Janice (first on Friends in 1994), Press F (Call of Duty, 2014), Fry
  (Futurama, 2010), Om Shanti Om, Sacred Games, DDLJ's father line and Stree. No song lyrics, and the
  top three lines of every card are spoiler-free.
- **Least certain, worth your eye:** card 9 (one fan-blog source says the line isn't in the film);
  card 30, "How's the josh?" / "High, sir!": I'm sure of the exchange, but the checker found no
  transcript and it was added after the check; card 26 (the exact full speech); card 23 (which
  episode); card 15 (which episode has the first knock).
- **One spoiler card:** 27 (Jaa Simran, jaa), done the safe way, with the safe meaning first.
- **Eras to confirm:** card 13 (2011, so 2010s & later), cards 16 to 18 and 28 (memes; Gru's plan is
  2018, Panik Kalm Panik 2020, Pawri 2021), card 5 (2012). Card 9 is the one non-film card, so its type
  is "Catchphrase".
- **Weakest as a "line":** card 16 (Roll Safe): people share the picture, not a phrase, so
  "tapping head meme" is its first search word. Cut it if you'd rather.
- **Search words were kept specific.** Bare titles of films and shows (Scam 1992, Chak De, Kuch Kuch
  Hota Hai, Om Shanti Om) were dropped so later cards from the same work can use them. Card 21's
  "sus" has no bare alias. Hinglish spellings are included (hai/hain, bhagwan/bhagwaan, pyaar/pyar).
  Apostrophe check: no card has two aliases that match once apostrophes are removed.
- **Real people and a viral creator:** card 23 (Scam 1992) is about a real scandal, described
  neutrally with no name. Card 28 names the creator of the Pawri clip, as the story needs; cut the name
  if you'd rather not. Roll Safe and Gru are not named as people.
- **Spoiler-safety of vague stories:** cards 22 (Om Shanti Om), 9 (Moody) and 29 (Stree) say only what
  the checker confirmed hides no twist. Card 22 doesn't mention reincarnation; card 29 doesn't mention
  what happens to the words on the wall.
- **Years** appear only inside spoiler warnings and in meme stories (cards 19, 28). None are stored on
  references.
- **Eras left out:** none of this batch pre-dates 1990, so every card has an era.
- **Not included on purpose:** "Wakanda Forever" (tied to a real loss), "Dormammu, I've come to
  bargain" and "Whatever it takes" (spoilers), and the Don and Deewar lines (pre-1990, so no era).
