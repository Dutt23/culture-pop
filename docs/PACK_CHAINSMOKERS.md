# "Before The Chainsmokers live" pack

Owner request, 2026-11-18: a concert pack for someone going to see The Chainsmokers who doesn't
know the songs: who they are, the hits a crowd sings back, and fan-favourite deep cuts. Cards live
in `src/data/seedPackChainsmokers.ts`; the pack is added once on install or on the v12 update. No
lyrics are quoted (they're copyrighted): cards describe each song, and every reference page now
has tap-only YouTube and Spotify search links under "Music and videos".

## Reading order

Who they are · Closer · Don't Let Me Down · Something Just Like This · Roses · Paris · #Selfie ·
High · Inside Out (deep cut) · Everybody Hates Me (deep cut).

## Facts and where they were checked (2026-11-18)

- Duo of Alex Pall and Drew Taggart, re-formed in New York in 2012; breakthrough #Selfie (2013/14);
  Roses (2015) their first US top 10; Don't Let Me Down (with Daya) won Best Dance Recording at
  the 59th Grammys; Closer (with Halsey, July 2016) No. 1 in the US and UK; Something Just Like
  This with Coldplay (2017); five albums: Memories...Do Not Open (2017), Sick Boy (2018), World
  War Joy (2019), So Far So Good (2022), Summertime Friends (2023); High (2022) led So Far So Good;
  Love Is Kind EP with Oaks (May 2026); went independent in October 2026:
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chainsmokers).
- Closer's 12 weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100: [Stereogum, The Number Ones](https://stereogum.com/2283569/the-number-ones-the-chainsmokers-closer-feat-halsey/columns/the-number-ones).
- Deep cuts are fan picks, not charts (Inside Out, Everybody Hates Me, Paris come up most often in
  fan lists and threads): [The Top Tens](https://www.thetoptens.com/music/chainsmokers-songs/),
  [r/The_Chainsmokers thread](https://redlib.belloworld.it/r/The_Chainsmokers/comments/1bs7tta/whats_your_favorite_chansmokers_song_from_each).
- Tour dates differ between ticket sites ([Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/the-chainsmokers-tickets/artist/1667791),
  [Songkick](https://www.songkick.com/artists/2867816-chainsmokers)), so no card states dates; the
  "who they are" example mentions Mumbai in December only as an example line.
