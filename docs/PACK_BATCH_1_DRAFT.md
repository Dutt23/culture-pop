# "You'll hear this" pack: batch 1 draft (30 cards)

Status: **draft for the owner's review.** Nothing here is in the app yet. After you approve it, the
cards ship as built-in entries through a new database version (the same way v7 refreshed the
built-in cards), marked as editor-written like the demo cards are today.

How to review: for each card, check that it's right, that the top three lines (Means, When people
say it, Say back) give nothing away, and that it's a line your circle actually says. Mark a card
**cut** or **change** and say how; everything else ships as written.

Each card has:

- **Means**: what it means when someone says it.
- **When people say it**: what the speaker is doing with it.
- **Say back**: a short line you can reply with.
- **Story**: where it comes from.
- **Context**: how people use it now.
- **Example**: a real-life moment.

Spoiler-heavy cards keep the safe meaning first. The spoiler meaning is hidden until you choose to
see it, and its "When people say it" line ends with "save the scene until you've seen it".

Mix: Disney and Pixar 6 · Marvel 4 · Harry Potter 3 · Friends and The Office 5 · memes 6 ·
Indian films and forwards 6. None repeat the 15 cards already in the app.

## Notes for the reviewer

- **Fact-checked once** by a separate checker with web lookups, and fixed. Least certain, worth your
  eye: card 25's say-back (written for the app, not a quote from the film) and the exact scene in
  card 26 (DDLJ).

  warnings, as in the existing Titanic and snap cards.
- **Sholay and Mr. India have no era**, because the app's eras start at the 1990s. They still show up
  in search and on Discover under Film.
- **Hinglish lines** use common spellings, with the usual variants as alternate spellings for
  search.
