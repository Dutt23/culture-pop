# "You'll hear this" pack: batch 2 draft (30 cards)

Status: **draft for the owner's review, not built in yet.** If approved, it would be built in as a
second pack file next to `src/data/seedPack1.ts`, shipped later by a new database version (the same
way batch 1 shipped with version 9), and marked as editor-written like the demo cards.

How to review: for each card, check that it's right, that the top three lines (Means, When people
say it, Say back) give nothing away, and that it's a line your circle actually says. Mark a card
**cut** or **change** and say how; everything else ships as written.

Each card has:

- **Means**: what it means when someone says it.
- **When people say it**: what the speaker is doing with it.
- **Say back**: a short line you can reply with.
- **Story**: where it comes from.
- **Context**: how people use it now.
- **Example**: a real-life moment.

Spoiler-heavy cards keep the safe meaning first. The spoiler meaning is hidden until you choose to
see it, and its "When people say it" line ends with "save the scene until you've seen it".

Mix: Disney and Pixar 4 · Marvel 3 · Harry Potter 2 · Friends, The Office and other sitcoms 5 ·
memes 7 · Indian films, shows and memes 9. None repeat the 45 cards already in the app, and none
reuse their search words.

## Notes for the reviewer

- **Fact-checked once** by a separate checker with web lookups, and fixed:
  - card 26 is from Dil To Pagal Hai, not Kuch Kuch Hota Hai;
  - card 28's "Hey Maa Mataji" is Daya's cry, not Jethalal's;
  - card 23's "mark" is the mark on Teja's cheek, not a target;
  - card 27's example no longer spells out the question, and the card is a meme rather than a quote;
  - card 7 no longer hints at how the Hulk stays in control;
  - card 29 dropped the "saala" search word.
  Confirmed: Dobby's film line ("Dobby is a free elf", 2010) and gravestone, the Pushpa dub lines,
  and the Binod, Two buttons and Evil Kermit origins. Least certain, worth your eye: the exact scene
  in card 23, and card 26 (the sources are lists, not the film itself).
- **Card 27 can't fully hide its twist.** The famous question itself says what happens, and it is
  also what people type, so it stays a search word. The card's title and its first meaning never
  say it. Cut the card if that's not good enough.
- **Eras to confirm:** card 11 (Unagi, which aired around 2000), card 12 (the episode "Fun Run", 2007, per
  the checker's memory, not a source), card 5 (Iron Man, 2008, so 2000s even though the spoiler meaning
  is from 2019).
- **Four spoiler cards,** all done the safe way: 5 (Iron Man), 6 (Spider-Man), 9 (Dobby) and 27
  (Kattappa). Card 27's title says "The Kattappa question" and not the full question on purpose, so
  the title doesn't give away what happened. The full question is the first search word, so
  searching for it still finds the card.
- **Where the origin is left out:** cards 19 (Two buttons) and 20 (Evil Kermit) say what the picture
  shows, but not which cartoon or film it came from. I wasn't sure enough to name one.
- **Search words were kept specific.** No card lists a bare everyday word ("always", "cool",
  "squirrel", "free", "wow"). Cards 7 and 13 use only the full phrase. "Unagi" is a real
  Japanese word for eel, so it may also come up for sushi searches; cut that alias if it bothers you.
- **Real people in memes** (cards 15 and 17) are described neutrally and not by name. Card 18's dog
  is a real dog, and not named.
- **Years** appear only inside spoiler warnings and in card 30's one-line story (same as batch 1's
  Rasode card). None are stored on references.
- **Eras left out:** none of this batch pre-dates 1990, so every card has an era.
- **Hinglish lines** use common spellings, with the usual variants as alternate search words.
- **Not included on purpose:** "Moye moye" (2024, and it comes from a song) and Gangs of Wasseypur
  (the famous lines are either revenge lines that hint at deaths or too strong for a safe card).
